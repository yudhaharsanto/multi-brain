## Multi Brain Startup

Before broader repo exploration, read `.multibrain/session.md` first and read its `## State` block — that is the authoritative current position. Then open only the named bucket in `.multibrain/indexes/` that matches the active task, and deeper `.multibrain/context/*.md` notes only when the bucket points to relevant detail. After meaningful work, prepend one line to the appropriate bucket, rewrite `## State`, and avoid duplicating an entry that already exists.
